Manufacturing process

1
Disperse CMC powder into glycerin. Wetting the thickener in glycerin prevents lumps before water contact.
Carboxymethyl celluloseGlycerin
2
Add warm deionised water and mix until the CMC fully hydrates and the base thickens evenly into a gel.
Deionised waterWarm water aids hydration
3
Add all Phase B surfactants and fold in gently. Avoid aggressive mixing to prevent excess foam formation.
HoliSurf LFSodium cocoyl glutamateCoco glucoside
4
Add preservative, then fragrance and mica to desired scent and colour. Added last to prevent heat degradation.
PreservativeFragranceMica
5
Adjust pH to 6.5–7.5 using citric acid or sodium hydroxide. Lower pH increases viscosity; target is skin-compatible near-neutral.
